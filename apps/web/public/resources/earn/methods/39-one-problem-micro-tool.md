# Starter kit · Method 39 · One-problem micro-tool

> Strength: Medium-high. That is our screening judgement of how well this method holds up for a beginner with $0, not an earnings forecast. Typical first-sale window: 30–90 days.

## The offer in one look
- Buyer: a niche calculator, formatter, checker, estimator or generator solving one repeated task.
- Deliverable: one-problem micro-tool, scoped to one buyer
- What AI does: requirements, coding assistance, tests and optional language interface.
- $0 start: static/local-first app on free hosting; pre-sell or deliver manually before adding paid infrastructure.
- Note: A real need comes first; code alone is not a business.

## First sale, step by step
Start here: make one private sample of the deliverable (one-problem micro-tool, scoped to one buyer) for one real prospect (buyer: a niche calculator, formatter, checker, estimator or generator solving one repeated task.) and send it to them personally.

1. Write your offer sheet for one narrow buyer (Build 01 helps). Name five real prospects you can reach without buying a list.
2. Make one private sample with invented or public information. Never use someone's private data without permission.
3. Send five personalised messages, one at a time, each with a reason that is specific to that person. No mass messages.
4. Offer a small, bounded first job with a clear price, timeline and revision limit.
5. Deliver, run the acceptance checks below, get written approval, and ask for an honest testimonial only after real work.

## Acceptance checks (proof to keep)
- [ ] The work delivers what the offer says: one-problem micro-tool, scoped to one buyer
- [ ] The client approved the work in writing
- [ ] No invented reviews, numbers or guarantees anywhere
