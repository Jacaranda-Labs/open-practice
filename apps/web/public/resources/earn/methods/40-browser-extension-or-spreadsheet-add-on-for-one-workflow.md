# Starter kit · Method 40 · Browser extension or spreadsheet add-on for one workflow

> Strength: Medium-high. That is our screening judgement of how well this method holds up for a beginner with $0, not an earnings forecast. Typical first-sale window: 30–90 days.

## The offer in one look
- Buyer: professionals repeatedly copying, formatting, checking or summarizing non-sensitive data.
- Deliverable: browser extension or spreadsheet add-on for one workflow, scoped to one buyer
- What AI does: coding/test assistance and optional bring-your-own-key features.
- $0 start: local/unlisted pilot; sell setup/support or pre-orders before store fees and hosted APIs.
- Boundary: least permissions, local processing by default, clear privacy notice.

## First sale, step by step
Start here: make one private sample of the deliverable (browser extension or spreadsheet add-on for one workflow, scoped to one buyer) for one real prospect (buyer: professionals repeatedly copying, formatting, checking or summarizing non-sensitive data.) and send it to them personally.

1. Write your offer sheet for one narrow buyer (Build 01 helps). Name five real prospects you can reach without buying a list.
2. Make one private sample with invented or public information. Never use someone's private data without permission.
3. Send five personalised messages, one at a time, each with a reason that is specific to that person. No mass messages.
4. Offer a small, bounded first job with a clear price, timeline and revision limit.
5. Deliver, run the acceptance checks below, get written approval, and ask for an honest testimonial only after real work.

## Acceptance checks (proof to keep)
- [ ] The work delivers what the offer says: browser extension or spreadsheet add-on for one workflow, scoped to one buyer
- [ ] Boundary kept: least permissions, local processing by default, clear privacy notice
- [ ] The client approved the work in writing
- [ ] No invented reviews, numbers or guarantees anywhere
